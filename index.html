Our story

It began in Ammi's kitchen, and it still tastes like it.

When I was a child, Ammi made biryani every Eid and every time a relative came to stay. My chachu used to say he could smell it from the lane. I learned by standing next to her, watching the onions turn golden and the handi get sealed.

We started Ammi's Biryani in Biratnagar because neighbours kept asking for it. We have no shop front. Everything is cooked in one kitchen, packed hot, and sent straight to you.
